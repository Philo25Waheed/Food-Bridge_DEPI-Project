How Food Bridge Works

Three straightforward steps that turn prospective waste into community nourishment.

1. Share a Donation

Food businesses or individuals post surplus food with photos, quantity, pickup windows, and safe handling notes in under 2 minutes.

2. Connect with a Charity

Verified shelters and food banks get instant notifications, discover nearby items via the interactive map, and request required allocations.

3. Make an Impact

Coordinate easy contactless or scheduled pickup, verify collection with digital codes, and view transparent environmental metrics.
